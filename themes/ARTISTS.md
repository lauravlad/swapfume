# Agent Swapfume: artist rotation

One artist per week, top to bottom. Agent Swapfume takes the **first artist without a ✅**, builds the theme,
then adds ✅ and the date. When every row has a ✅, it starts again from the top with a fresh take
(different colours, background and avatars from the last time).

Only artists who died before 1956 are listed (their work is in the public domain in most countries),
and the themes are *inspired by* their style: original drawings, never copies of real paintings.
The order alternates dark and light themes and keeps neighbouring weeks very different.
Laura can reorder, add or delete rows at any time.
