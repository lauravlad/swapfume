### Description
```
Swapfume is a small, friendly community for swapping perfume. Trade samples, decants and full bottles, and meet at community meetups, with fellow fragrance lovers in the United States, Canada and Romania (members swap within their own country). No money, ever: just generosity and good packing.

HOW IT WORKS
• List what you own and mark what you'd trade.
• Add the perfumes you're hoping to find to your wishlist.
• Swapfume finds two-way matches: people who have what you want and want what you have.
• Propose a swap. Offer several samples for one rarer decant if you like.
• Ship by mail, or meet in person if you're within 20 miles of each other.
• Confirm when your package arrives, then review each other.

A CATALOG MADE FOR PERFUME LOVERS
• Thousands of perfumes from designer, niche, Middle Eastern and classic houses.
• Top, heart and base notes, perfumers and scent families.
• Member reviews: stars, longevity, sillage, best seasons and time of day.
• Suggest a missing perfume or a correction, and add bottle photos.

SHARE YOUR SWAP SHELF
Make a beautiful picture of what you're trading, in a style that matches your taste (floral, woody, amber & oud, fresh, gourmand and more), with ready-made text for Facebook, Instagram or Reddit.

GIFTS
Feeling generous? Give a perfume to another member with nothing asked in return.

PRIVATE AND SAFE
• Your address is shared only with your swap partner, and only after you both accept.
• Your postal code is never shown. Members see only a rounded distance and a blurred map of your general area.
• Reviews and completed-swap counts help you swap with confidence.
• Report or block anyone, any time.
• For adults 18 and over.

Swapfume is run by Octopup Studio. Questions? hello@swapfume.com
```

## 3. App Privacy (left sidebar → Trust & Safety → App Privacy)

**Privacy Policy URL:**
```
https://swapfume.com/#/privacy
```

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** → **Yes**

For every item below, answer:
- **Linked to the user:** Yes
- **Used for tracking:** No
- **Purpose:** App Functionality (add "Customer Support" for Customer Support)

| Data type | Why |
|---|---|
| Contact Info → **Email Address** | Sign in, account emails |
| Contact Info → **Name** | Display name, and the name on the package |
| Contact Info → **Physical Address** | Shipping address, shown only to a swap partner |
| Location → **Coarse Location** | Postal code, for rounded distances and nearby swaps |
| User Content → **Photos or Videos** | Profile and bottle photos |
| User Content → **Other User Content** | Reviews, swap notes, listings, wishlist |
| User Content → **Customer Support** | Messages to hello@swapfume.com |
| Identifiers → **User ID** | Your account ID |

**Not collected:** payment info, health, contacts, browsing or search history, precise location, usage data, diagnostics, advertising data. Swapfume has no ads and no tracking.

> ⚠️ **Date of birth.** The app is meant to keep only the fact that a member is 18+. Right now the birthdate typed at sign-up stays stored with the account login details. Fix this before submitting (one small SQL step), so the Privacy Policy and these answers stay true.
